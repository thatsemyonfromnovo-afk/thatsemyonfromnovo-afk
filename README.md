### Hi, I'm Semen 👋

**Market risk analyst at VTB · MSc student in Platform Economics at Saint Petersburg State University**

I turn routine risk analytics into reliable Python pipelines — and explore how AI agents can take over the rest.

#### 🏦 What I do at work
- **PFE automation.** Built a Python pipeline that reads deal memos in Word, normalizes FX and commodity forwards, computes potential future exposure (PFE) and limit usage, and writes results back with an audit trail. Ambiguous inputs stop with a clear error — in risk, a silent mistake costs more than a stop. Covered by 126 regression tests.
- **Historical PFE add-ons.** Automated loading of Bank of Russia rates and MOEX ISS quotes and the calculation of add-on coefficients across 12 tenor buckets for FX and commodity instruments (incl. INR, AED and wheat), with separate long/short tails.

#### 🚀 Projects
- **AI prompter for personal bankers** — T-Bank «Invest. Premarket» 2026, team *keepers of tokens*. A real-time assistant that listens to a client call, pulls portfolio and client context and suggests the next answer or a clarifying question; the banker stays in control. *(private repo)*
- **Master's research** — a risk-based model for choosing automation technology for internal banking processes with generative AI.
